# The World in One Day — 24 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news.

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) — $100, 50 places.

## The day's stories

- **Lead — Three tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz.** Iran's
  Revolutionary Guard says three oil tankers hit naval mines and caught
  fire in the world's busiest oil chokepoint, as attacks on shipping reach
  their highest level since the war began.
- **Korea — Seoul scrambles jets as Russian planes near.** South Korea sent
  fighter jets up after Russian military planes entered its air-defence
  zone; an evacuation warning for Ulleung island was lifted after three
  minutes, with no airspace breached. Seoul also names a new foreign
  minister and intelligence chief, and the US naval blockade of Iran —
  which Tehran vows to break — holds Gulf oil exports near a standstill.
- **Lighter — Beaver Supermoon meets the Seven Sisters.** November's full
  moon peaks at 9:53 a.m. EST and slides within 0.9° of the Pleiades
  before dawn, the first of three supermoons in a row.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
The image itself was drawn with PIL; the repo ships the finished image.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
